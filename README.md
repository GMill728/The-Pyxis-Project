A thousand years ago, a temporal rift swallowed enemy ships on the edge of a black hole.  Caught mid-war and blindsided by the disaster, the crews lay frozen in time ever awaiting their fate.  For a millenia, the time-warping properties of the black hole have suspended them on the verge of destruction, but now the rift is collapsing. In response colonies sent you, Pyxis, an android prototype on a mission to explore the armada and recover their logs of the cataclysm.  Your mission is simple, board the alien vessels, fight anything that resists, and get out.  With your circratronic brain you don’t have the same luxuries of virtual immortality.  You only have minutes till the wreckage is crushed into oblivion and the colonies’ fate sealed.
