# The-Pyxis-Project



## Description
A procedurally generated retro movement shooter made collaboratevly for a capstone class at the Kutztown University of Pennsylvania. Early Access released on May 4, 2026.

## Story Overview
In the 22nd century, the Federal Republic of Sol (FRS) created a synthetic black hole called "The Anchor" in the T Pyxidis system, using it as a near-limitless energy source.  This discovery turned the FRS into a dominant industrial and militant superpower, allowing it to conquer much of the galaxy through brute force and coercion.

By the late 23rd century only a few factions remain:

- 0Pz -  A decentralized anarchist rebellion spreading chaos and resistance.
- NULL - Inorganic androids seeking the eradication of organic material.
- Reclaimed - FRS Prisoners turned zombified war machines sent to "maintain order."

You are Pyxis, A new generation of AI and cybernetics sent to defend The Anchor.  The 0Pz plan to turn it into a black hole bomb capable of destroying the entire galaxy. With all factions converging on T Pyxidis and the original scientists dead, you must eliminate all threats and collect ship's logs for relay to the FRS station.  Remember, you are expendable, The Anchor is not.
