Long ago in the 22nd century amidst a grueling war, a group of scientists from the Federal Republic of Sol (or FRS) used a modified version of the 20th century hydrogen bomb to create a small black hole.  To protect their work from spies and the enemies of the FRS, the research station was in a little-known binary star system known as T Pyxidis (T Pyx).  The nova star at the center of this system created a hostile environment but allowed them to avoid detection amidst the high doses of radiation.  There, the scientists were able to tow the black hole to a stable orbit around the nova star, establishing the first stable synthetic black hole and a shift in the scales of power.  They named this invention: "The Anchor".

The first and most known faction is the 0pposition or 0Pz.  They're a splinter-celled anarchist rebellion to the Federal Republic of Sol.  Communicating in decentralized radio bursts, the 0Pz spread a simple message: "Resist oppression, embrace chaos."  Seen as the greatest threat, the 0Pz have transcended a political party and have become an unkillable ideal.  Their sole belief and core strategy is that so long as the FRS exists, so too will people who denounce and oppose it and the more systems that the FRS concurs, the larger their numbers and stronger their resolve.
